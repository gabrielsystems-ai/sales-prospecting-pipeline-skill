# Cold outreach — fictional examples

All businesses, observations and drafts are invented. No messages have been sent.

## Weak: unsupported diagnosis and oversized pitch

“Your outdated site is losing customers. Your team must waste hours on admin. We can rebuild everything with a CRM, AI, memberships, payments and dashboards. Can we book an hour tomorrow?”

Problems: invented loss and workload, unnecessary criticism, unvalidated replacement, too many ideas and excessive commitment.

## Better: website entry

Hi, I was looking at the fictional Fernway Garden Services site and noticed the service pages don't include an enquiry link. I had an idea for making it easier for visitors to request the right service from their phone. Could I send you a short outline?

## Better: connected journey with a good existing website

Hi, I was exploring the fictional Oakmere Makers Studio and saw that workshops and returning-member sessions use separate booking links. I had an idea for bringing those routes into one clearer journey while keeping your booking provider behind it. Would you like me to send the outline?

Separate links are an observation, not proof of a failing system.

## Better: email

Subject: An idea for Fernway's service enquiries

Hi,

I was looking through the fictional Fernway Garden Services pages and noticed visitors need to return to Contact to enquire. I had an idea for putting the relevant enquiry step alongside each service, especially on mobile. Could I send a short outline to whoever manages the website?

## Checks

- Each useful draft is approximately 35–75 words, excluding the email subject.
- Use a published channel preference. A phone number alone does not confirm WhatsApp.
- Do not repeat the permission CTA after the prospect requested the idea.
- Never include real private contact details in public adaptations.
